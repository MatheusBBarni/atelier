# ADR-002: V1 product shape — shared contract + early-abort, phased sibling migration

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-06-15

## Context

ADR-001 reframed this packet as a governance spine the sibling packets consume. PRD-phase research then established a sharper, decisive fact: **both siblings are already fully specced** (PRD + techspec + tasks + ADRs), and they already own the spine's two headline assets — `subtask-dag-execution` owns the plan IR (`ExecutionGraph`) and whole-plan consent gate; `approval-trust-list` owns the hard irreversible-action floor (fires even in `yolo`) and the rich decision-support card. The spine cannot "own these from scratch"; the open question is how it adds value without reopening two finished packets.

Clarifying answers set the V1 shape: (1) the spine's job is a **shared contract + the net-new capability, with phased sibling migration**; (2) it unifies the **decision card + plan legibility**; (3) the new capability is a **single-agent turn-1 early-abort** showing the interpreted goal + approach + agent + write-scope; (4) success is **outcome-based with a dual-alarm** on intervention rate.

## Decision

Ship governance-spine V1 as:
- A **shared governance-decision contract** — one decision-card model (action, target, blast-radius, reason, risk label, decision affordances) that every governance pause renders through, plus a **minimal shared plan/intent legibility model** so a single-agent intent echo and a DAG graph are presented consistently.
- The **single-agent turn-1 early-abort** (the net-new capability): complexity-gated, non-binding; before any write it shows the orchestrator's interpreted goal + approach + chosen agent + write-scope, and the user Accepts (proceed) or Rejects (abort, optional redirect).
- **Outcome-based, dual-alarm metrics** (revert/rework/kept rate + intervention-rate calibration that alarms on both extremes), local-only.
- **Phased migration:** `approval-trust-list` and `subtask-dag-execution` adopt the shared contract in a later phase — **not** a V1 retrofit of their shipped specs.

The spine **references, does not reimplement**, the siblings' assets: the hard irreversible floor stays in `approval-trust-list`; the DAG IR/scheduler stays in `subtask-dag-execution`.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Unify-by-refactor now

- **Description**: Extract a shared primitive + IR and migrate both already-specced packets onto it in V1.
- **Pros**: Fullest "spine"; eliminates duplication immediately.
- **Cons**: Reopens two completed packets' techspecs and tasks; high coordination risk and rework.
- **Why rejected**: Disrupts finished work for consistency that can be achieved incrementally.

### Alternative 2: Just the new capability

- **Description**: Ship only the single-agent early-abort; drop the spine framing.
- **Pros**: Smallest, lowest risk.
- **Cons**: Leaves the duplication and future-divergence risk unaddressed; abandons the unifying value the owner chose.
- **Why rejected**: Misses the portfolio benefit of one coherent governance UX.

### Alternative 3: Consolidate into the spine

- **Description**: Fold the siblings' overlapping mechanisms into the spine as sole owner.
- **Pros**: Single owner, cleanest end state.
- **Cons**: Largest restructure; highest risk to shipped specs.
- **Why rejected**: Disproportionate disruption for V1.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Delivers a genuinely net-new user capability (catch a misread goal before any work) while setting the consistency standard, with minimal disruption to finished packets.
- Outcome-based metrics avoid rewarding the rubber-stamping the evidence warns about.

### Negative

- The de-duplication benefit is deferred to a later phase; duplication persists in the interim.

### Risks

- **Coordination/sequencing** with two in-flight siblings — mitigated by defining the contract first and migrating on each sibling's own schedule.
- **The early-abort may not earn its keep** (devils-advocate's residual doubt) — mitigated by complexity-gating and measuring its catch rate as a proceed-gate.

## Implementation Notes

Reuse the existing clarification pause/resume + event→chat projection for the early-abort. The shared contract is a presentation + lifecycle convention the siblings conform to over time, not a forced V1 rewrite.
